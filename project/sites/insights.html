What Is a City Budget — and Why Should You Care?

Civic Education 8 min read March MMXXVI

Every year, your city decides how to spend hundreds of millions of dollars — on streets, schools, libraries, parks, fire trucks, water lines. That decision is called a budget. It is not a technical document for accountants. It is a public statement of priorities, written in numbers.
