§ III.
A Practice Statement

The premise is simple: public money belongs to the public, and the public has the right to read it.

For more than a decade, the work has been the same — to design public-finance systems that function, and to write about them in language that any thoughtful citizen can follow. Each budget is a public document. Each financial report is a public document. The question is not whether they can be read; it is whether they will be.

This practice exists to make the reading possible.

— Eliana Faria Lima
