What Is a City Budget — and Why Should You Care?

Civic Education N.º 01 8 min read

Every year, your city decides how to spend hundreds of millions of dollars — on streets, schools, libraries, parks, fire trucks, water lines. That decision is called a budget. It is not a technical document for accountants. It is a public statement of priorities, written in numbers.

This essay walks through how a city budget is built, who has a voice in it, and what a citizen can actually see by reading one.

About Lumina

Strategic intelligence for public administrators.

Lumina Smart Strategies LLC bridges the gap between complex public finance data and actionable governance. We provide high-level consulting, auditing, and digital transformation for municipalities.

Led by Founder Eliana Faria Lima, a specialist in public finance and government budgeting, our team consists of CPA-licensed auditors, digital transformation consultants, and transparency platform designers dedicated to building resilient public institutions.

The goal is simple: clear financial models, rigorous compliance, and the conviction that public money must be managed with absolute integrity.
