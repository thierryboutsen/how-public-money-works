## Editorial angle

```yaml
coreQuestion: "What does a local government audit examine, and how can a resident read the report without assuming more than the audit actually says?"
uniqueAngle: "Treat the audit report as a bounded evidence document: identify who performed the work, the period, objectives, scope, criteria, opinion or conclusions, findings, and follow-up."
whatThisArticleWillNotCover:
  - "Accusations about any government, official, employee, auditor, or contractor."
  - "A legal definition of audit that applies in every state or locality."
  - "A promise that an audit proves the absence of fraud, waste, error, or misconduct."
  - "Technical training on auditing standards or governmental accounting."
  - "A ranking of governments by audit outcome."
jurisdictionScope: "Conceptual U.S. local-government overview. Audit authority, required frequency, engagement type, report language, and access path must be described as variable."
examplesAllowed:
  - "A generic walkthrough of a report cover, scope paragraph, opinion or conclusion, findings, recommendations, management response, and follow-up."
  - "Common audit-type labels, clearly qualified as non-universal."
examplesToAvoid:
  - "Real findings or allegations without the complete official report and context."
  - "Treating an unmodified opinion as a clean bill of health for all operations."
  - "Calling every review, inspection, investigation, or agreed-upon procedure an audit."
```
