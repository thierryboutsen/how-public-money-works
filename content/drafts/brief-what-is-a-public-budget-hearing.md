## Editorial angle

```yaml
coreQuestion: "When a local government announces a budget hearing, what can a resident reasonably expect to learn, and how should they prepare without assuming the rules are the same everywhere?"
uniqueAngle: "Organize the article around a three-part resident workflow: verify the local rules, read the right documents, and ask a focused question that can be followed after the hearing."
whatThisArticleWillNotCover:
  - "National rules for notice, speaker time, registration, remote participation, voting, or budget adoption."
  - "Legal advice about open-meeting or public-hearing rights."
  - "A claim that every local government must hold the same kind or number of hearings."
  - "A promise that public comment will change the proposed budget."
  - "A general introduction to what a city budget is."
jurisdictionScope: "Conceptual U.S. local-government overview. The draft must repeatedly direct readers to state law, local charter or code, official notice, agenda, and governing-body rules for operative procedure."
examplesAllowed:
  - "Generic examples of questions about a proposed service change, revenue assumption, reserve use, or implementation measure."

  - "Specific notice periods, speaking limits, hearing counts, adoption dates, or vote thresholds."
```
