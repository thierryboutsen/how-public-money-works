# Visual Anti-Patterns — Do Not Use

The following are not acceptable final article covers:

- flat emergency SVG placeholders;
- generic infographic cards;
- simple icon grids presented as finished editorial art;
- dashboard or admin-interface screenshots;
- stock-office photography;
- executives posing in corporate environments;
- floating dollar signs, raining money or piggy-bank imagery;
- embedded article titles or explanatory labels;
- partisan or campaign imagery;
- direct reuse of another article's master image without an approved override;
- low-detail compositions that visibly break the established Insights grid.
